The numbers.

Last time we told you where we're going. This time: what it actually costs to get there.

We're raising £30,000. Here's the honest breakdown of where it goes.

Venue fit-out ~£14,000
Electrical, plumbing, changing area, the works. Making Robinson's Brewery actually work as a sauna venue.
 
Cold plunge ~£4,000
A proper one, not a wheelie bin. Temperature-controlled, built to last.
 
First 3 months operating ~£8,000
Rent, utilities, insurance. Keeping the lights on while we build the customer base.
 
Contingency ~£4,000
Because something always costs more than you thought.

Why crowdfunding and not a bank loan?

A loan means debt from day one, and pressure to make numbers work before we've had time to figure anything out. Crowdfunding means the people who want this to exist are part of making it happen — which feels more honest about what this actually is.

It's also all or nothing. Either we hit £30k and it happens, or we don't and everyone gets their money back. No half-measures, no "we'll figure it out as we go."
