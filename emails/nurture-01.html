The Build · Issue 01
 

How this started.

This is the first email in a short series about what we're building and why. No fluff. Just the real version.

It started as a pop-up. We had two barrel-tent saunas, a cold plunge, a field, and no idea whether anyone in Stockport would bother showing up on a wet Saturday morning to sweat in a tent and jump in freezing water.

They did. Enough of them that we thought — maybe there's something here.

The original idea was simple: make sauna accessible. Not a luxury spa with a waiting list and a locker system. Just heat, cold water, and people. £15 a session. Show up.

We ran it as a proof of concept — a thing that needed to exist before we could argue it should be permanent. And it worked well enough that we're now trying to make it permanent.

"Robinson's Brewery, Stockport. A real space, a real kitchen, a courtyard that fits what we've been building toward."

That's where we're heading. A permanent venue at Robinson's Brewery. Not a franchise, not a chain, not a brand with a manifesto. A sauna you can go to every weekend without booking a spa day.

We're funding it through a crowdfunder — and in the next email, we'll tell you exactly what the money is for.
